The Problem with Traditional Training Management

NYC DOB training providers operate in a high-stakes regulatory environment. A single compliance gap — a missing identity check, an unlogged hour, an unsigned document — can invalidate an entire cohort of certificates and expose your organization to fines, license suspension, or worse.

Most organizations manage this with a combination of spreadsheets, paper records, and generic LMS platforms that were never designed for regulatory compliance. The result is always the same:

Audit exposure

When an inspector arrives, staff scramble to reconstruct evidence that should have been automatically logged from day one.

Identity gaps

No reliable way to verify that the person who studied is the person who enrolled — a key requirement for online training programs.

Manual reconciliation

Hours are tracked in one system, identity documents in another, certificates in a third. Staff spend hours every week reconciling records.

Certificate validity risk

Certificates issued without meeting every legal requirement can be challenged or revoked — damaging your reputation and your students' careers.

What RECS Does

RECS is a single unified platform where your organization administers, delivers, monitors, and certifies training programs that lead to State and Federal licenses — with compliance enforced at the system level, not the process level.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the certificates legally valid before the DOB?

Yes. RECS only issues a certificate when every configured legal requirement is verifiably met — hours, identity, assessments, and enrollment. The Validation Snapshot freezes all evidence at issuance, immutably, ready for any audit.

Which licenses and programs does it support?

The multi-tenant architecture supports multiple licenses, specialties, courses, and sub-schools from a single control panel. Compliance rules are configured once per license and the system enforces them automatically across every program.

How long does implementation take?

It depends on the number of licenses and catalog size, but the platform is delivered already configured and operational. The process is Discovery → Configuration → Operation, and Shlomo handles all the technical work.

Is my organization's data mine?

Yes. Your students, certificates, and audit dossiers belong to you and are exportable as PDF or spreadsheet at any time. Nothing is locked into the platform.

Do I need technical staff to run it?

No. RECS is built for non-technical staff and ships with complete operational documentation in English and Spanish. Your team administers, delivers, and certifies from a simple interface.
