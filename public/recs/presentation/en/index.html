03 / 11 What we built

What is RECS?

The system your organization uses to administer, deliver, supervise and certify the programs that lead to State and Federal licenses. Everything follows a clear structure:

License

The legal rules: validity, daily hour limit, manager and required verifications.

Specialty

The accreditation: minimum hours, years of validity and credits (CEU).

Modules & Lessons

The content: organized videos and texts, with their assessments.

Courses

The vehicle through which the student enrolls and learns.

Certificate

Issued only when hours, attendance and identity are met.

Active Proctoring: profile photo, facial recognition, ID, email, phone and address.
Tamper-proof audit trail for authorities: evidence is sealed the moment the certificate is issued.

06 / 11 Where we're at our best

Total control: no one cheats here

Study monitoring

  • Live photo every ~5 minutes, at random moments: the student never knows when, so it can't be gamed.
  • Counts real time every 15 seconds and detects if they step away. Only genuine study time is added.
  • Daily hour limit: when the cap is reached, counting stops and resumes the next day, as the law requires.

Cheat-proof assessments

  • Limited attempts: once used up, it locks. No infinite exams.
  • Mandatory minimum score: no one passes without reaching the required grade.
  • Maximum time per exam: when it runs out, it closes and grades itself, with the reason on record.

Everything is saved: start and end time, every attempt and every verification. When the certificate is issued, RECS freezes all the evidence and no one can alter it.

RECS watches every minute: identity, real time and exams with limited attempts and time. No one studies for another or passes without meeting the bar, and all evidence is sealed.

Your data in the vault, not under the mattress
